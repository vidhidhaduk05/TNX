Figure 2. Kaplan-Meier cumulative incidence of cognitive outcomes over 5 years following carotid artery stenting (CAS) versus carotid endarterectomy (CEA) in the primary propensity score-matched cohort (4,822 matched pairs). (A) Cognitive decline; (B) dementia; (C) composite of cognitive decline or dementia. In each panel, the blue line represents the estimated cumulative event probability for CAS and the red line represents that for CEA; shaded areas indicate the corresponding 95% confidence intervals. Hazard ratios (HRs), 95% confidence intervals, and p values are shown within each panel. Number-at-risk tables are provided below each plot. Curves are shown over 1,825 days (5 years) of follow-up.
